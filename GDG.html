About the community

A place to learn by building, together.

We’re a student-led tech community at MMMUT. We make it easier to try a new tool, ask a question without judgment, find teammates and take an idea beyond the classroom.

Our mission

Make practical tech learning easier to start.

Host approachable sessions, share what students are learning and create room to practise through small, useful projects.

Our vision

A campus where curiosity has a team behind it.

Help students across years and branches find mentors, collaborators and confidence to contribute to the technology around them.

What you can expect

Small steps count — from your first Git command to a project you can show.

  • Hands-on workshops

    Learn by trying the tools, not just watching slides.

  • Senior mentorship

    Get help with the first steps, stuck bugs and next goals.

  • Real project experience

    Work with a team, make decisions and finish something useful.

  • Career readiness

    Build confidence with collaboration, demos and a portfolio of work.
